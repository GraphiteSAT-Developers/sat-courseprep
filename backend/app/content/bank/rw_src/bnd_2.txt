## bnd medium
P: The fictional photographer Imani Brooks spent three years documenting life in a single apartment ______ she photographed its residents at birthdays, holidays, and ordinary breakfasts.
Q: Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?
A: building,
*A: building—
A: building
A: building, and,
X: The second independent clause explains the first, and a dash can join two independent clauses this way. A comma alone creates a comma splice, no punctuation creates a run-on, and "and," with a comma after it is incorrect.
