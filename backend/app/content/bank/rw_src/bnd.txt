## bnd easy
P: Honeybees perform a "waggle dance" to tell other bees where to find ______ the direction and length of the dance show the location of the flowers.
Q: Which choice completes the text so that it conforms to the conventions of Standard English?
A: food, the
*A: food. The
A: food the
A: food and the
X: Each part ("Honeybees perform...food" and "the direction...flowers") is an independent clause, so they need to be separated properly. A period does that. A comma alone creates a comma splice, no punctuation creates a run-on sentence, and "and" without a comma before it is also not standard for joining these two long independent clauses.
