## wic medium
I: The following text is from a 1909 short story. The narrator is describing a small town.
P: The town had a way of absorbing every newcomer within a year or two, so that by the time a stranger had learned the names of the streets, the streets seemed to have learned his name as well.
Q: As used in the text, what does the word "absorbing" most nearly mean?
A: soaking up liquid
*A: taking in and making part of itself
A: holding the full attention of
A: reducing the force of
X: The town takes newcomers in so completely that they come to belong there, so "absorbing" means taking in and making part of itself (B). The text isn't about liquid (A). "Holding the attention of" (C) and "reducing the force of" (D) don't fit the idea of newcomers becoming part of the town.
