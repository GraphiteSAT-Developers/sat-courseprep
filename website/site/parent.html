Connect your student

  1. Your student signs in to Graphite SAT and opens Account, then Parent access.
  2. They press Get a parent code and send you the 8-character code. It works once, for 7 days.
  3. Enter it here. You'll see their progress, study time and plan, but not their individual answers.
