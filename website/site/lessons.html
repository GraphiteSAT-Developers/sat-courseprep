Learn it by doing it

There's a lesson for every Reading and Writing question type, in the order they come on test day: words in context and text structure, evidence and inferences, punctuation and grammar, then transitions and notes. Each one has you sort, match, spot the mistake or build the answer before anything is explained, and comes back a few days later as a quick recall check.

There's a lesson for every skill on SAT Math, plus the Desmos course and a few lessons on how memory works. No two play the same way: some give you three hearts, some put you on the clock, some are a story or a case to crack, and most have you drag, tap or build something before anything is explained. A few days later each lesson comes back as a quick recall check, so it sticks.
