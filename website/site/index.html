A tutor that asks before it tells

Open the tutor beside any question, lesson or Level up set, at lunch or the night before the test. It knows what you've missed this week, and it gives the help in three steps instead of handing over the answer.

  1. Step 1

    A nudgePoints at the part of the question you skipped or misread.

  2. Step 2

    The methodNames the approach, like setting up a percent-change equation, without doing it for you.

  3. Step 3

    The full solutionEvery step, plus why each wrong choice was tempting. Always one tap away.

The Desmos moves that save the most time

Bluebook puts Desmos next to every Math question, and most students only use it to multiply. Our 14-lesson course teaches the moves that solve or check a third to half of the Math section, and when doing it by hand is faster.

  1. Graph both sidesAny equation, any degree. Type each side, then click where the graphs cross.
  2. The tilde trickAsked for a, b and c in an equivalent expression? A list of x-values and one regression finds all three.
  3. Regression from a tableTurn data into a line or an exponential model in one line of typing.
  4. Sliders for unknown constantsDrag k until the lines go parallel, then confirm it by hand in ten seconds.
  5. Nine more, and a checklistSmall habits like checking angle mode and brackets, so a right method doesn't give you a wrong answer.

Made for the long stretch to test day

Most SAT prep doesn't fail on the first day. It fails in week three, when practice slips and nobody notices. These are the things that keep it going.

Streaks

Miss a day, keep the streak

One missed day a week is covered by a streak freeze, automatically. A streak should reward showing up, not punish a busy Tuesday.

Reminders

One nudge, at your hour

Pick the time in Settings. You get at most one reminder a day, and none on a day you've already practiced.

Plan

Every day has one job

The plan counts down to your test date and sizes each day to the time you said you have, so you never start by wondering what to do.

Parents

A weekly report for parents

Study time, scores and what's next, by email every week, plus an alert if practice stops. Students can see exactly what parents see.

Breaks

Pause instead of quitting

Exams, a trip, a busy season: pause the plan for 30 days and nothing is billed. Your history and plan are there when you come back.

Why Graphite SAT exists

Most SAT prep hands you a pile of questions and a score at the end. The score tells you how you did. It doesn't tell you why you picked C.

We built Graphite SAT around that question. Every wrong answer gets a reason, every reason turns into practice, and the practice keeps coming back until the mistake stops showing up. It's slower than skimming another thousand questions. It works better.

Questions parents and students ask

How much will my score go up?

We don't promise a number of points, because gains depend on where you start and how much time you put in. We do promise that it goes up if you do the work: the score-up promise lists every condition. Your dashboard shows your practice scores after each test, so you can see what's working within the first three weeks.

How early should we start?

Eight to twelve weeks before the test works well for most students. If your test is closer, Graphite SAT shortens the plan and puts more time on the domains with the most points available.

Does the tutor just give out answers?

No. It starts with a hint, then the method, and shows the full solution only when you ask for it. Students who work through the hints remember the method on the next question.

Can parents see progress?

Yes. On Sharpened and Masterstroke, a parent gets a weekly email with practice time and practice test scores. Students can see exactly what parents see.

How do the free trial and canceling work?

Any account can try Sharpened free for 7 days from the subscription page. There's no card, and it switches back to the free plan by itself. Paid plans can be paused for 30 days or canceled from the same page, in two taps; a canceled plan keeps working until the end of what you paid for.

What device do I need?

Daily sets work on a phone, tablet, Chromebook or laptop. Take full practice tests on a laptop, Chromebook or tablet, since that's what you'll use in Bluebook on test day.

Is Graphite SAT the official SAT?

No. Graphite SAT is independent and isn't affiliated with the College Board. Our practice tests follow the published structure of the digital SAT. For official practice tests, use the College Board's Bluebook app alongside Graphite SAT.

What do you do with student data?

We use it to run the tutor and build the study plan. We don't sell it, and we don't show ads. The privacy policy lists everything we collect.
