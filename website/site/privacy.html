Privacy policy

This policy explains what Graphite Learning, Inc. ("Graphite", "we") collects when students and parents use graphiteprep.com and the Graphite app, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

  5. Who we share it with

  9. Changes to this policy

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, grade, planned test date, target score, and a password stored as a salted hash.
  • Practice data: the questions you answer, your answers, time spent, hints used, and practice test scores.
  • Tutor conversations: messages you send to the AI tutor and its replies.
  • Parent details: a parent or guardian's name and email when a student adds them for progress emails.
  • Billing details: handled by our payment processor. We see the card brand, last four digits and expiry date, never the full card number.
  • Usage data: pages visited and buttons clicked, collected only if you accept analytics cookies. See the cookie policy.

2. How we use it

We use this information to run your account, choose your daily practice, explain mistakes, send progress emails you've asked for, process payments, answer support requests, and keep the service secure. We don't sell personal information and we don't show advertising.

3. The AI tutor and your data

Your answers and tutor messages are sent to our AI provider to generate explanations. Our contract with that provider prohibits it from using student data to train its models. We review a small sample of anonymized tutor conversations to check explanation quality.

4. Students under 18

Graphite is built for high school students. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we'll delete it. Parents and guardians of students under 18 can ask to see, correct or delete their student's data at any time.

5. Who we share it with

  • Service providers that host the site, process payments, send email and run the AI tutor, under contracts that limit their use of the data.
  • A parent or guardian the student has added, who receives progress summaries.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect someone's safety.

We don't share data with colleges, the College Board or advertisers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account and practice data while your account is open. If you close your account, we delete it within 30 days, except billing records we must keep for tax purposes.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, download or delete your data. Depending on where you live, including California and other US states, you may have additional rights under state law. We won't treat you differently for using them. Email privacy@graphiteprep.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, data is encrypted at rest, and staff access is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure, so we'll notify you promptly if a breach affects your information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll email account holders and update the effective date at the top of this page at least 14 days before it takes effect.
