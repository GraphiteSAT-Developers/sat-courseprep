What's on the parent dashboard

  • ProgressPractice test scores, the skills getting stronger, and where the easiest points are.
  • Study timeMinutes this week against the goal, days practiced, and a streak.
  • AlertsA heads-up when practice stops for a few days or a score drops.
  • Plan and billingChange, pause or cancel the plan, and see every change in one place.
