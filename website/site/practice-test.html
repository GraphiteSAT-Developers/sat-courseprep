    Directions

    Every question here comes with something short to read: a passage, two related passages, a student's notes, or a table or graph. Read it, then answer using only what it says or shows.

    Each question has four choices and one best answer. Select words in a passage and press Annotate to highlight them or leave yourself a note.

    This section covers algebra, advanced math, problem solving and data analysis, and geometry and trigonometry. The calculator works on every question, and the reference sheet has the common formulas.

    Unless a question tells you otherwise, assume that:

    • every number and variable is a real number;
    • figures are flat and drawn to scale;
    • a function can take any real input that gives a real output.

    Multiple-choice questions have one right answer. For the rest, type your answer in the box; the left side of the screen shows how to enter it.
