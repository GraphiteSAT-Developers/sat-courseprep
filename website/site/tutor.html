How hints work

  1. A nudge. A question back to you, so you find the next step yourself.
  2. The method. Which approach fits, by hand or in Desmos, without doing it for you.Where in the passage to look and which rule or question type it is, without giving the answer away.
  3. The full solution. Every step (or the exact words in the passage), plus why each wrong choice is tempting. A similar question comes back later to check it stuck.
