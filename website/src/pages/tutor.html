How hints work

  1. A nudge. A question back to you, so you find the next step yourself.
  2. The method. Which approach fits, by hand or in Desmos, without doing it for you.
  3. The full solution. Every step, plus why each wrong choice is tempting. A similar question comes back later to check it stuck.
