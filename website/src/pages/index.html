The tutor

Hints first. Answers when you've earned them.

Looking at a worked solution feels like learning. Working it out yourself is what raises scores. The tutor gives you three levels of help, one at a time.

  1. Level 1

    A nudgePoints at the part of the question you skipped or misread.

  2. Level 2

    The methodNames the approach, such as setting up a percent-change equation, without doing it for you.

  3. Level 3

    The full solutionEvery step, plus why each wrong choice is tempting.

FAQ

Questions parents and students ask

How much will my score go up?

We don't promise a number. Gains depend on your starting score, how many weeks you have, and how many days you practice. Your dashboard shows your score after each practice test, so you can see what's working within the first three weeks.

How early should we start?

Eight to twelve weeks before the test works well for most students. If your test is closer, Graphite shortens the plan and puts more time on the domains with the most points available.

Does the tutor just give out answers?

No. It starts with a hint, then the method, and shows the full solution only when you ask for it. Students who work through the hints remember the method on the next question.

Can parents see progress?

Yes. On Daily and Coached plans, a parent gets a weekly email with practice time, practice test scores, and the skills being worked on. Students can see exactly what parents see.

What device do I need?

Daily sets work on a phone, tablet, Chromebook or laptop. Take full practice tests on a laptop, Chromebook or tablet, since that's what you'll use in Bluebook on test day.

Is Graphite the official SAT?

No. Graphite is independent and isn't affiliated with the College Board. Our practice tests follow the published structure of the digital SAT. For official practice tests, use the College Board's Bluebook app alongside Graphite.

What do you do with student data?

We use it to run the tutor and build the study plan. We don't sell it, and we don't show ads. The privacy policy lists everything we collect.
