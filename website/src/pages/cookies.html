Cookie policy

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Graphite SAT uses as few as possible, and analytics cookies only run if you say yes.

On this page

  1. Types of cookies
  2. Cookies we use
  3. Managing cookies
  4. Questions

1. Types of cookies

  • Essential cookies keep you signed in, protect forms from abuse and remember your cookie choice. The site doesn't work without them, so they're always on.
  • Analytics cookies count page views and button clicks so we can see which pages help students. They're off until you accept them.

We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

3. Managing cookies

Use the button at any time to turn analytics on or off. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will stop sign in from working.

Email privacy@graphiteprep.com. Our privacy policy explains how we handle personal information more broadly.
