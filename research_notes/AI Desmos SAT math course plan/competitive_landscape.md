# Competitive Landscape: AI-Powered SAT Math Prep and Desmos-Focused SAT Courses (US, as of Oct 2026)

Method note: research was done with web search only. Direct page fetches were blocked by the network proxy (acely.com, 1600.io, satdesmos.com and satified.org all failed), so the findings below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. Search returned no Reddit threads. Most sources are vendor pages or vendor-published "best of" lists. Treat feature and price claims as self-reported and re-check them before publishing.

## 1. AI SAT tutors/platforms (2026): features, AI capability, adaptivity, pricing, sentiment

### Takeaway
The free baseline has risen a lot. Khan Academy (official, with Khanmigo) and, since January 2026, Google Gemini (full-length tests built with The Princeton Review) are both free or nearly free. In June 2026 The Princeton Review also put a free SAT app inside ChatGPT. Many small AI-first startups (Acely, LearnQ, AlphaTest, Archer, DoublePrep, Mentis, Miyagi Labs, Crackd, Aniko, Makon) compete on question banks plus a chat tutor. The incumbents (UWorld, Kaplan, Princeton Review) still charge roughly $59 to $2,999.

### Cited Findings
**Free and official tier**

- Khanmigo is listed at about $4/month for individuals and free through participating districts. It uses a Socratic approach (guiding questions and hints, not answers) — [devtoollab](https://devtoollab.com/ai-tools/khanmigo); [Acely blog comparison (vendor)](https://acely.com/blog/best-sat-prep-options-2026)
- PrepMaven's review (updated Aug 2026) says Khan's resources "are much less helpful for advanced students (scoring above 700 in each section)" and that Khan offers no real 1:1 help — [PrepMaven](https://prepmaven.com/blog/test-prep/khan-academy-sat-prep-review/)
- A claimed "+120 points" Khan/College Board 2025 study could not be verified — [kidsaitools](https://www.kidsaitools.com/en/articles/ai-study-tools-for-teens-exam-prep) (unverified)
- Google launched free full-length SAT practice tests in Gemini in January 2026, built with The Princeton Review, with instant feedback and tutoring — [TechCrunch, 2026-01-22](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/22/google-now-offers-free-sat-practice-exams-powered-by-gemini); [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/education/practice-sat-gemini/); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/ai-platforms-assistants/gemini/gemini-now-offers-full-length-sat-practice-tests-for-free)

The spread probably reflects QBank vs. full course — [PrepGenix](https://prepgenix.com/blog/best-sat-prep-platforms-compared/); [PracticeTestGeeks](https://practicetestgeeks.com/sat/uworld-sat); [Brighterly](https://brighterly.com/blog/best-sat-prep-courses/)

### Inferences
- "AI tutor plus question bank" is now commodity and free at the low end (Khan/Khanmigo, Gemini, Princeton Review in ChatGPT). A paid product has to win on accuracy and on depth at the top end (700 to 800), not on having AI.
- The startup field is crowded and lightly differentiated. Most products claim the same things: an adaptive diagnostic, a Socratic tutor and Desmos tips.

### Gaps

## 2. Desmos-specific SAT courses and creators

### Takeaway
There are many Desmos-specific offerings, but they are mostly cheap or free video or lesson courses ($0 to $25/mo, or $240 for a live cohort). Several are bundled inside broader memberships (1600.io, PrepPros, OnePrep). Creator reach is concentrated on TikTok and YouTube, but reliable follower counts were not obtainable.

### Cited Findings
- 1600.io "Mastering Desmos for the Digital SAT" is available only with a membership — [1600.io](https://1600.io/p/mastering-desmos-for-the-digital-sat)
- SATDesmos (satdesmos.com) costs $25/month, $60 for 3 months or $192/year — [SATDesmos](https://www.satdesmos.com/pages/home)
- PrepPros SAT Math Course includes Desmos explanations for hundreds of questions, with a $19.99/month option. Its advanced practice course is sold separately for $75 — [PrepPros](https://www.preppros.io/sat-math-course)
- OnePrep "SAT Math & Desmos Masterclass" is self-paced, with 5 modules, 48 lessons and 46 practice sets. No price was found — [OnePrep](https://www.oneprep.com/course/sat-math-walkthrough-videos)
- Cheetah Prep Desmos course is free to start; a Pro plan unlocks the full course, walkthroughs and analytics — [Cheetah Prep](https://www.cheetahprep.com/desmos)
- Outschool live class "SAT Math Prep: Practice, Strategies & Desmos Skills" costs $30/class, or $240 for 8 classes, for ages 14 to 17 — [Outschool](https://outschool.com/classes/sat-math-prep-practice-strategies-desmos-skills-6HsdBFIn)
- Aniko.ai offers a free Desmos course of 32 interactive lessons with Desmos embedded in each lesson — [Aniko](https://www.aniko.ai/free-resources/sat/courses/desmos); [Aniko roadmap](https://www.aniko.ai/blog/how-to-learn-desmos-for-the-sat)
- Free options include Udemy courses ("Desmos for the New Digital SAT Math Test", "Digital SAT Math Exam Explained – Desmos in Action"), PeakPrep SAT on Skool (a free community and course), and a YouTube "Digital SAT Math Desmos Course" playlist — [Udemy 1](https://www.udemy.com/course/desmos-for-digital-sat/); [Udemy 2](https://www.udemy.com/course/digital-sat-exam-explained-desmos-in-action/); [Skool PeakPrep](https://www.skool.com/peakprep-sat-1551/classroom/67ed023f?md=792f87daab2d494db7d46bc96b7fb3e9); [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLf3ypEs9Kobgascv5bwpOadB0UiVI5IQS)
- Gumroad and Substack creators: MathChops Substack (Desmos intro and DSAT analysis), and "learnsatmath" on Gumroad — [MathChops](https://mathchops.substack.com/p/a-five-minute-dsat-desmos-intro); [Gumroad learnsatmath](https://learnsatmath.gumroad.com/l/groundup)
- TikTok creators include James Lu SAT (@jameslusatprep, Desmos tricks; one listing shows "478.6K" without saying whether that is views or followers), scottsSATprep, and @rafinhasworld (one Desmos tips video with 37.3K likes) — [TikTok James Lu](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/james-lu-sat-desmos-guide); [TikTok #desmos](https://www.tiktok.com/tag/desmos); [TikTok student-desmos](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/student-desmos)
- Free Desmos cheat sheets and guides are widespread as content marketing (Acely, EdisonOS, LearnQ, AlphaTest) — [Acely cheat sheet](https://acely.com/sat-prep/desmos-cheat-sheet); [EdisonOS guide](https://www.edisonos.com/digital-sat/desmos-guide); [LearnQ blog](https://blogs.learnq.ai/math-calcutor-desmos-digital-sat/)

### Inferences
- Standalone Desmos content is cheap to the point of commoditization. Willingness to pay for "Desmos tricks" alone is probably capped at about $20 to $25/month, judging by SATDesmos, PrepPros and 1600.io.
- Free Desmos guides are being used as SEO lead magnets by almost every AI startup. A Desmos-first positioning will face direct SEO competition.

### Gaps
- No YouTube subscriber counts, Discord member counts or verified TikTok follower counts were obtainable. Next step: check channels manually (e.g., James Lu SAT, Scalar Learning, SupertutorTV, and r/Sat or SAT Discord servers).
- OnePrep pricing was not found.

## 3. Does any competitor's AI actually operate Desmos? Evidence of AI Desmos/math mistakes

### Takeaway
No evidence was found of any SAT product whose AI tutor drives a live Desmos graph, for example by programmatically plotting, adjusting sliders or reading off intersections inside the tutor conversation. Competitors either embed a calculator that the student operates or have the AI give text advice on whether to graph. One competitor says explicitly that its AI "does not run Desmos for you." This looks like a real white space, though absence of evidence from search is not proof.

### Cited Findings
- Satified describes its AI as advising whether to graph or solve by hand while the student runs Desmos, and says the AI "does not operate Desmos for the student" — [Satified](https://satified.org/blog/desmos-plus-ai-sat-math/)
- Aniko embeds a Desmos calculator in its lessons that the student operates. Mentis describes an AI tutor guiding step by step "while you practice in Desmos." Crackd has Desmos "embedded everywhere." None states that the AI controls the graph — [Aniko](https://www.aniko.ai/blog/how-to-learn-desmos-for-the-sat); [Mentis](https://mentisprep.com/desmos-calculator-sat); [Crackd](https://crackd.it/)
- 1600.io provides pre-recorded "Desmos graph explanations," which are static or video, not AI — [1600.io](https://1600.io/)
- No source found says whether any product calls the Desmos API programmatically (search summary of results).
- AI math errors in general: Education Week reports that chatbots "regularly answer math questions incorrectly" and give contradictory answers — [EdWeek, Sept 2024](https://www.edweek.org/teaching-learning/ai-gets-math-wrong-sometimes-how-teachers-deal-with-its-shortcomings/2024/09)
- In a study of about 1,000 high schoolers, GPT-4 chat use raised practice scores but lowered exam scores once the AI was removed. The AI's step-by-step solution approach was wrong 42% of the time — [Hechinger Report](https://hechingerreport.org/kids-chatgpt-worse-on-tests/)
- Gemini's SAT tests were reported to contain off-syllabus, unsolvable or multi-answer math items — [MentoMind](https://mentomind.ai/gemini-sat-prep-review/); [CollegeTestCoach](https://www.collegetestcoach.com/blog/gemini-sat-prep-review)
- A critique of "AI SAT prep" from a tutor's perspective — [Robinovitz Substack](https://jasonrobinovitz.substack.com/p/the-illusion-of-ai-sat-prep)
- Experienced tutors warn against "playing with Desmos" on hard problems without a plan, because students flounder; Desmos should be used deliberately — [MathChops](https://mathchops.substack.com/p/a-five-minute-dsat-desmos-intro)

### Inferences
- An AI that renders and manipulates an actual Desmos state could do several things text tutors cannot. It could show the exact keystrokes and graph, verify its own answer by computation (for example, intersection points) instead of reasoning in text, and grade the student's Desmos work. This addresses the well-documented weakness of LLM arithmetic and reasoning errors.
- The risk: no documented student complaints specifically about AI tutors getting Desmos wrong were found. The pain point is inferred from general AI math-error evidence, not observed.

### Gaps
- Direct tests of Khanmigo, Gemini or ChatGPT giving Desmos keystroke instructions were not found. A hands-on audit is recommended.
- Desmos API licensing terms for commercial embedding and AI control were not checked. Desmos Studio offers an API, but its commercial terms need verification.

## 4. Pricing tiers, business models, market size

### Takeaway
Prices fall into four bands: free/freemium (Khan, Gemini, Princeton Review's ChatGPT app, many startup free tiers), low-cost subscriptions ($4 to $25/month: Khanmigo, 1600.io, SATDesmos, PrepPros), time-boxed course or QBank access ($59 to $449: UWorld, Kaplan On Demand, Princeton Review self-paced), and premium live or tutored courses ($799 to $2,999+, or $420/hr). The SAT audience is about 2M takers per year. Test-prep market estimates vary widely by definition, and no SAT-specific market size was found.

### Cited Findings
- Price points are as listed in Section 1 and Section 2, plus DoublePrep's pay-per-use credits — [DoublePrep](https://www.doubleprep.com/ai-tools-sat-practice)
- More than 2 million students in the class of 2025 took the SAT, the first time above 2M since 2020. 97% took it digitally and 68% tested on a school day — [College Board newsroom](https://newsroom.collegeboard.org/sat-participation-class-2025-surpasses-2-million-test-takers-first-time-2020)
- The Khan Academy and College Board partnership has reached 10M+ students on official SAT practice — [Khan blog](https://blog.khanacademy.org/college-board-khan-academy-mark-10-million/)
- Technavio forecasts the US test-prep market to grow by $19.78B over 2025 to 2030 at an 8.0% CAGR. This is growth, not total market size — [Technavio](https://technavio.com/report/test-preparation-market-industry-in-the-us-analysis)
- IBISWorld puts US Testing & Educational Support at $39.2B in 2026, a much broader category — [IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/market-size/testing-educational-support/1549/)
- Global estimates conflict sharply: $74.2B (exam prep and tutoring, 2026) vs. $542M (test prep, 2026) — [Research and Markets / TBRC](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/5807127/exam-preparation-tutoring-market-report); [Global Growth Insights](https://www.globalgrowthinsights.com/market-reports/test-preparation-market-122464)

### Inferences
- The relevant TAM is narrow: students aiming for 700+ in math. A rough bottom-up estimate is a fraction of the ~2M annual takers times $50 to $300 per student. This is my estimate, not sourced.
- Business-model norm: monthly subscription or time-boxed access with a free diagnostic. Seasonal demand around test dates favors monthly or 3-month passes over annual plans.

### Gaps
- No credible SAT-only US market size was found.
- The share of takers scoring 700+ in math was not retrieved. It is available in the College Board national report percentile tables.

## 5. Student complaints and the path to 750–800 math

### Takeaway
The recurring complaints are that official material runs out, Khan is too easy above 700, third-party questions are badly calibrated, and Bluebook tests feel easier than the real hard module 2. High-score advice centers on deliberate Desmos fluency plus algebra fundamentals and drilling hard-module questions. Reddit-sourced evidence could not be retrieved, so everything here is anecdotal or from vendors and tutors.

### Cited Findings
- Bluebook has a limited supply of full-length tests; one source cites eight available tests and no announced Test 12. Retakes are inflated by memory, although adaptive routing means a single sitting shows only about two-thirds of a test's questions — [TheTestAdvantage](https://thetestadvantage.com/blog/how-many-practice-tests-does-bluebook-have); [SigmaPrep](https://www.sigmaprep.io/blog/out-of-bluebook-practice-tests)
- Students ask where to find more practice after Bluebook and note that there are fewer official tests than in the paper era — [Khan Academy support forum](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/community/posts/23253738086157-Digital-SAT-Practice-Tests)
- "Many unofficial tests have questions that are either far too easy... or confusingly hard" — [ArborBridge](https://blog.arborbridge.com/digital-sat-six-ways-to-prep)
- Perception that the real test is harder than Bluebook: Piqosity speculates that the College Board found its practice tests too easy, while others attribute the gap to the hard module 2 — [Piqosity](https://www.piqosity.com/2025/02/14/digital-sat-practice-test-announcements/); [AlphaTest](https://www.alphatestai.com/blog/9-does-the-bluebook-practice-test-feel-too-easy-heres-the-truth-about-the-real-digital-sat); [SAT Crash Course](https://thesatcrashcourse.com/are-the-sat-practice-tests-on-bluebook-accurate/)
- Khan is "much less helpful for advanced students (above 700)" — [PrepMaven](https://prepmaven.com/blog/test-prep/khan-academy-sat-prep-review/). Khan "gets repetitive" — [Scribd SAT guide](https://www.scribd.com/document/802123461/SAT-guide). A commenter finished Khan and plateaued at 1380–1390 (anecdotal) — [Spike News Substack](https://spikenews.substack.com/p/lets-talk-free-sat-prep-3-top-resources)
- Path-to-800 tactics from tutors: use Desmos only when you know how you will use it; be able to solve with and without Desmos; learn sliders (e.g., a parameter giving exactly one intersection), regression from tables, and mean = median with an unknown variable — [MathChops Desmos intro](https://mathchops.substack.com/p/a-five-minute-dsat-desmos-intro); [MathChops analysis guide](https://mathchops.substack.com/p/digital-sat-math-analysis-guide); [MathChops resources](https://mathchops.substack.com/p/sat-summer-studying-resources)

### Inferences
- High scorers need (a) a supply of realistically hard module-2 items, (b) fast, deliberate Desmos technique, and (c) error diagnosis that separates careless slips from concept gaps. A course built on these three directly targets the gaps the complaints describe.

### Gaps
- r/Sat and r/SATprep threads could not be retrieved, so first-hand "how I got 800" posts and UWorld vs. 1600.io sentiment are missing. Manual Reddit review is recommended.
- App Store and Trustpilot ratings for UWorld, Khan, Acely and others were not retrieved.

## 6. Differentiation opportunities and risks

### Takeaway
The clearest open space is an AI that actually operates and verifies with a live Desmos graph, focused on the 700-to-800 band and on hard-module calibration. The main risks are free incumbents (Google, Khan, Princeton Review in ChatGPT) adding the same features, AI accuracy failures, and Desmos licensing or College Board dependence.

### Cited Findings
- No competitor found with AI-driven Desmos manipulation; the closest explicitly disclaims it — [Satified](https://satified.org/blog/desmos-plus-ai-sat-math/)
- The free AI competition is strong and backed by large incumbents: Gemini with Princeton Review (Jan 2026), Princeton Review's ChatGPT app (Jun 2026), Khanmigo at about $4/month — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/22/google-now-offers-free-sat-practice-exams-powered-by-gemini); [Princeton Review](https://www.princetonreview.com/press/princeton-review-chatgpt-sat-app); [devtoollab](https://devtoollab.com/ai-tools/khanmigo)
- Accuracy is the known failure mode of AI prep (Gemini errors; GPT-4 solution approaches wrong 42% of the time) — [MentoMind](https://mentomind.ai/gemini-sat-prep-review/); [Hechinger](https://hechingerreport.org/kids-chatgpt-worse-on-tests/)
- AI chat help can lower unaided exam performance (crutch effect), while hint-based tutor designs avoided the drop — [Hechinger](https://hechingerreport.org/kids-chatgpt-worse-on-tests/)

### Inferences
- **Opportunities:**
  - An AI that drives an embedded Desmos (plots, sliders, regressions) and uses computed results to check its own answers.
  - A Desmos-or-algebra decision trainer: when to graph, keystroke efficiency, timed drills.
  - A curated or generated hard module-2 bank with validation, to fix calibration complaints.
  - "Replay the expert's Desmos keystrokes" on official Bluebook-style items.
  - Score-band focus at 700–800 math, where Khan is weakest.
  - Price around $15 to $25/month, or a $79 to $149 test-cycle pass, to undercut the incumbents.
- **Risks:**
  - Google, Khan or College Board could add live-Desmos AI quickly.
  - Desmos API commercial terms are unverified.
  - AI-generated items with errors damage trust; human or programmatic validation is needed.
  - The crutch effect, if the AI does the graphing for the student. The design should make the student reproduce the keystrokes.
  - Desmos content is commoditized and SEO-saturated, so differentiation must be the interactive AI, not the tips.
  - Seasonal demand and churn.

### Gaps
- No user research on willingness to pay for AI-operated Desmos.
- No head-to-head accuracy benchmark of existing AI tutors on hard SAT math items was found. Building one would also make good marketing material.
