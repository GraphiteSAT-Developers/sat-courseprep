### 1. psda-coffee-beans-per-ounce

*Easy · Multiple choice · route: hand-primary · techniques: T9*

A 12-ounce bag of coffee beans costs 9.60 dollars. What is the cost, in dollars, per ounce of the coffee beans?

- **A.** $0.08$
- **B.** $0.80$
- **C.** $1.25$
- **D.** $115.20$

**Answer:** B

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** It's given that 12 ounces cost 9.60 dollars. The cost per ounce is the cost divided by the number of ounces: 9.60/12 = 0.80. Therefore, the coffee beans cost 0.80 dollars per ounce. Distractors: 1.25 is 12/9.60, the number of ounces per dollar (the rate inverted); 0.08 misplaces the decimal point in 0.80; 115.20 multiplies the cost by the number of ounces instead of dividing.

**Desmos** (not yet verified in Desmos). Type:<br><code>\frac{9.60}{12}</code>

The line shows 0.8. One division by hand is just as fast; Desmos only confirms it.

</details>

### 2. psda-direct-proportion-y-x

*Easy · Student-produced response · route: hand-primary · techniques: T9*

The value of $y$ is directly proportional to the value of $x$. When $x = 8$, $y = 30$. What is the value of $y$ when $x = 20$?

**Answer:** 75

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** It's given that y is directly proportional to x, so y = kx for some constant k. Substituting x = 8 and y = 30 yields k = 30/8 = 15/4. Therefore, when x = 20, y = (15/4)(20) = 75. Alternate approach: x is multiplied by 20/8 = 2.5, so y is also multiplied by 2.5, and 30 · 2.5 = 75.

**Desmos** (not yet verified in Desmos). Type:<br><code>f(x)=\frac{30}{8}x</code><br><code>f(20)</code>

The second line shows 75. Setting up the proportion by hand is faster; use Desmos to check.

</details>

### 10. psda-growth-year

*Medium · Multiple choice · route: desmos-primary · techniques: T1*

A town's population was 2,400 at the start of 2015 and has grown by 6% each year since then. If this growth continues, at the start of which year will the population first exceed 4,000?

- **A.** $2023$
- **B.** $2024$
- **C.** $2025$
- **D.** $2027$

**Answer:** B

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** Need 1.06^n > 4000/2400 = 5/3 ≈ 1.667. Square repeatedly: 1.06^2 = 1.1236, 1.06^4 = 1.1236^2 ≈ 1.2625, 1.06^8 ≈ 1.2625^2 ≈ 1.594 (too small), and 1.06^9 ≈ 1.594 · 1.06 ≈ 1.689 (large enough). So n = 9 and the year is 2015 + 9 = 2024. Distractors: 2023 stops at n = 8, the last year the population is still below 4,000; 2025 counts 2015 as year 1, adding an extra year; 2027 assumes linear growth of 6% of 2,400, or 144 per year (1,600/144 ≈ 11.1, so 12 years).

**Desmos** (not yet verified in Desmos). Type:<br><code>y=2400(1.06)^x</code><br><code>y=4000</code>

The intersection is at x ≈ 8.77, so the population passes 4,000 during the 9th year. At the start of 2015 + 9 = 2024 it is 2400(1.06)^9 ≈ 4,055.

</details>
