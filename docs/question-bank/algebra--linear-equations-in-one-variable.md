### 1. alg-decimal-quarter-coefficients-solve

*Easy · Multiple choice · route: desmos-primary · techniques: T1*

If $0.25x + 1.5 = 0.75x - 2.5$, what is the value of $x$?

- **A.** $-8$
- **B.** $-2$
- **C.** $2$
- **D.** $8$

**Answer:** D

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** Subtracting 0.25x from both sides of the given equation yields 1.5 = 0.5x - 2.5. Adding 2.5 to both sides yields 4 = 0.5x. Dividing both sides by 0.5 yields x = 8. Therefore, the value of x is 8. Distractors: -8 results from a sign error, writing -0.5x = 4 after moving the x-terms to the left side; -2 results from subtracting 2.5 instead of adding it, which gives 0.5x = -1; 2 results from multiplying 4 by 0.5 instead of dividing by 0.5.

**Desmos** (not yet verified in Desmos). Type:<br><code>y=0.25x+1.5</code><br><code>y=0.75x-2.5</code>

Click the intersection of the two lines: (8, 3.5). The solution is the x-coordinate, 8.

</details>

### 4. alg-flour-batches-equation

*Easy · Multiple choice · route: hand-primary · techniques: —*

A bakery starts the day with 40 kilograms of flour and uses 2.5 kilograms of flour for each batch of bread. Let $b$ be the number of batches made when 12.5 kilograms of flour remain. Which equation represents this situation?

- **A.** $40 - 2.5b = 12.5$
- **B.** $40 + 2.5b = 12.5$
- **C.** $2.5 - 40b = 12.5$
- **D.** $12.5 - 2.5b = 40$

**Answer:** A

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** It's given that the bakery starts with 40 kilograms of flour and uses 2.5 kilograms for each batch, so after b batches it has used 2.5b kilograms and has 40 - 2.5b kilograms left. It's also given that 12.5 kilograms remain, so 40 - 2.5b = 12.5. Therefore, the equation is 40 - 2.5b = 12.5 (which gives b = 11 batches). Distractors: 40 + 2.5b = 12.5 adds the flour used instead of subtracting it; 2.5 - 40b = 12.5 swaps the amount used for each batch with the starting amount; 12.5 - 2.5b = 40 swaps the starting amount with the amount that remains.

</details>

### 12. alg-trail-crew-solution-meaning

*Easy · Multiple choice · route: hand-primary · techniques: —*

A trail crew has completed 250 meters of a new trail and adds 18 meters of trail each week. The equation $250 + 18w = 970$ represents this situation, where $w$ is a number of weeks. Which of the following is the best interpretation of the solution to the given equation?

- **A.** The number of meters of trail the crew adds each week.
- **B.** The number of weeks the crew needs to add 250 meters of trail.
- **C.** The number of weeks the crew needs to complete 970 meters of trail.
- **D.** The number of meters of trail the crew will have completed after 970 weeks.

**Answer:** C

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** It's given that the crew has completed 250 meters and adds 18 meters each week, so after w weeks the completed length is 250 + 18w meters. Setting this equal to 970 means the equation asks when the trail reaches 970 meters. Subtracting 250 from both sides yields 18w = 720, and dividing both sides by 18 yields w = 40. Therefore, the solution, 40, is the number of weeks the crew needs to complete 970 meters of trail. Distractors: 'the number of meters of trail the crew adds each week' is the coefficient 18, not the solution; 'the number of weeks the crew needs to add 250 meters' treats 250, the length already completed, as the target; 'completed after 970 weeks' reads the total 970 as a number of weeks rather than as a length in meters.

</details>

### 13. alg-bookstore-split-copies

*Medium · Multiple choice · route: hand-primary · techniques: —*

A shipment of 150 copies of a book is divided between two bookstores so that one store receives 24 more copies than the other. How many copies does the store that receives more copies get?

- **A.** $63$
- **B.** $75$
- **C.** $87$
- **D.** $99$

**Answer:** C

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** Let c be the number of copies the store with fewer copies receives. Then the other store receives c + 24 copies, and since the two stores receive 150 copies in all, c + (c + 24) = 150. Combining like terms yields 2c + 24 = 150, so 2c = 126 and c = 63. Therefore, the store that receives more copies gets 63 + 24 = 87 copies. Distractors: 63 is the number of copies the other store receives; 75 is half of 150, which ignores the 24-copy difference; 99 adds 24 to 75 instead of to 63.

</details>

### 17. alg-count-solutions-identity

How many solutions does the equation $5(x - 2) - 3x = 2(x + 4) - 18$ have?

- **A.** Zero
- **B.** Exactly one
- **C.** Exactly two
- **D.** Infinitely many

**Answer:** D

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** Expanding the left side yields 5x - 10 - 3x = 2x - 10. Expanding the right side yields 2x + 8 - 18 = 2x - 10. Both sides are the same expression, 2x - 10, so the equation is true for every value of x and has infinitely many solutions. Distractors: 'Exactly one' is what a student expects from a linear equation without simplifying both sides; 'Zero' comes from adding 18 instead of subtracting it on the right side, 2x + 26, which leaves 2x - 10 = 2x + 26 with no solution; 'Exactly two' cannot happen for a linear equation.

Only one line appears, because the two graphs lie exactly on top of each other; clicking anywhere on it gives a point on both. Every value of x is a solution: infinitely many.

### 18. alg-count-solutions-x-zero-trap

*Medium · Multiple choice · route: either · techniques: T1*

How many solutions does the equation $2(3x - 4) = 6x - 8 + x$ have?

- **A.** Zero
- **B.** Exactly one
- **C.** Exactly two
- **D.** Infinitely many

**Answer:** B

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** Distributing on the left side of the given equation yields 6x - 8, and combining like terms on the right side yields 7x - 8. So the equation is 6x - 8 = 7x - 8. Subtracting 6x from both sides and adding 8 to both sides yields 0 = x. The equation is true for x = 0 only, so it has exactly one solution. Distractors: 'Zero' confuses the solution x = 0 with having zero solutions; 'Exactly two' is never possible for a linear equation whose x-coefficients differ; 'Infinitely many' comes from noticing 6x - 8 on both sides and overlooking the extra x on the right.

The two lines have different slopes and cross once, at (0, -8). One intersection point means exactly one solution. Simplifying by hand is about as quick.

</details>

### 24. alg-le-consecutive-twice-greatest

*Medium · Multiple choice · route: either · techniques: T1*

For three consecutive integers, twice the greatest integer is 23 less than 3 times the sum of the other two integers. What is the greatest of the three integers?

- **A.** $4$
- **B.** $6$
- **C.** $7$
- **D.** $8$

**Answer:** D

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** Let n be the least of the three integers, so the integers are n, n + 1 and n + 2. Twice the greatest is 2(n + 2), and 3 times the sum of the other two is 3(n + n + 1) = 6n + 3. Therefore, the greatest integer is 8. Distractors: 4 reverses '23 less than', writing 2(n + 2) = 23 - 3(2n + 1), so n = 2 and the greatest is 4; 6 is the least integer; 7 is the middle integer.
