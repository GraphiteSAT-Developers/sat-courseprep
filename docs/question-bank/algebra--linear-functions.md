### 1. alg-balloon-descent-initial-altitude

*Easy · Multiple choice · route: hand-primary · techniques: —*

The function $B(t) = 520 - 65t$ models the altitude, in meters, of a hot-air balloon $t$ minutes after it begins descending. Which of the following is the best interpretation of 520 in this context?

- **A.** The balloon's altitude is 520 meters when it begins descending.
- **B.** The balloon's altitude decreases by 520 meters each minute.
- **C.** The balloon reaches the ground 520 minutes after it begins descending.
- **D.** The balloon's altitude is 520 meters 65 minutes after it begins descending.

**Answer:** A

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** In the model B(t) = 520 - 65t, the value of B(0) is 520, so 520 is the altitude, in meters, when t = 0, that is, when the balloon begins descending. Distractors: 'decreases by 520 meters each minute' describes the coefficient of t, which is 65, not 520; 'reaches the ground 520 minutes after' confuses 520 with the solution of B(t) = 0, which is t = 8; '520 meters 65 minutes after' swaps the roles of the two constants, since B(65) is 520 - 65(65), a negative number.

</details>

### 2. alg-drain-rate-interpretation

*Easy · Multiple choice · route: hand-primary · techniques: —*

The function $w(t) = 420 - 15t$ gives the amount of water, in liters, remaining in a tank $t$ minutes after it starts draining. Which of the following is the best interpretation of 15 in this context?

- **A.** The tank contains 15 liters of water when it starts draining.
- **B.** The tank is empty 15 minutes after it starts draining.
- **C.** The tank loses 420 liters of water every 15 minutes.
- **D.** The amount of water in the tank decreases by 15 liters each minute.

**Answer:** D

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** It's given that w(t) = 420 - 15t, where t is the time in minutes. For each increase of 1 in t, the value of w(t) changes by -15, so the amount of water in the tank decreases by 15 liters each minute. Distractors: 'contains 15 liters of water when it starts draining' confuses the rate with the starting amount, w(0) = 420; 'empty 15 minutes after it starts draining' confuses the rate with the time to empty, which is 420/15 = 28 minutes; 'loses 420 liters of water every 15 minutes' swaps the roles of 420 and 15.

</details>

- **B.** $4$

- **D.** $12$

### 15. alg-lf-slope-through-two-points

*Easy · Multiple choice · route: hand-primary · techniques: T5*

The function $g$ is linear, and in the $xy$-plane the graph of $y = g(x)$ contains the points $(2, 9)$ and $(6, 1)$. What is the slope of this graph?

- **A.** $-2$
- **B.** $-\frac{1}{2}$
- **C.** $\frac{1}{2}$
- **D.** $2$

**Answer:** A

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

Distractors: -1/2 divides the change in x by the change in y; 1/2 does that and also drops the sign; 2 drops the sign, as if the outputs increased.

### 17. alg-lf-uneven-table-slope

*Easy · Multiple choice · route: desmos-primary · techniques: T5*

A table for the linear function $p$ gives these pairs $(x, p(x))$: $(-2, 13)$, $(1, 4)$, and $(7, -14)$. What is the slope of the graph of $y = p(x)$ in the $xy$-plane?

- **A.** $-9$
- **B.** $-3$
- **C.** $-\frac{1}{3}$
- **D.** $3$

**Answer:** B

<details><summary>Solutions</summary>

**By hand.** The slope is the change in p(x) divided by the change in x between any two pairs. Distractors: -9 is the change in p(x) between the first two rows, not divided by the change in x of 3; -1/3 divides the change in x by the change in p(x); 3 drops the negative sign, but p(x) decreases as x increases.

The regression lists m = -3 and b = 7, and R^2 = 1 confirms all three pairs lie on one line. The slope is m = -3. The inputs jump by 3 and then by 6, so the change from one row to the next is not the slope.
